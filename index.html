THE SEVEN MOVES FRAMEWORK

Applied Academic Case Study: Online Fitness Business Strategy

Human-AI Collaborative Research

Structured 7-Stage Strategic Dialogue

This document showcases the sequential execution of The Seven Moves Framework. By transitioning from high-stakes questioning to predictive horizon scanning, hypothesis calibration, and master prompt synthesis, this dialogue demonstrates how AI operates as an advanced strategic thinking partner.

1

Move 1: Ask a Real Question (High Stakes)

Baseline Inquiry
MY QUESTION TO AI Polished Academic Speech

"Can online fitness coaching operate as a fully viable, highly profitable business model today, allowing an individual to earn a complete full-time living exclusively online without relying on physical gym facilities?"

AI STRATEGIC ADVICE & RESPONSE Viability: High

Yes, online coaching is exceptionally viable and structurally superior to traditional gym models. By replacing geographic limitations and low hourly wages with high-ticket recurring subscriptions ($200–$500/month), an individual coach requires only 20 to 30 active clients to generate a $60,000–$120,000 annual net income.

Key Drivers: ~90% profit margins, zero facility lease overhead, scalability via digital client management tools.

2

Move 2: Drill Deeper (Evidence & 5–10 Year Time Horizon)

Horizon Scanning
MY QUESTION TO AI Polished Academic Speech

"Looking ahead over a 5 to 10-year horizon as AI and automated digital tools advance, will human coaches remain essential, or will AI tools completely replace human coaches in running online fitness businesses?"

AI STRATEGIC ADVICE & RESPONSE Shift: Hybrid Automation

AI will commoditize generic workout planning, but it will NOT replace high-ticket human accountability. Over the next decade, standalone automated fitness apps will drop to near-zero pricing. However, human coaches who integrate AI as a backend copilot will thrive by focusing on emotional intelligence, psychological motivation, and personal brand connection.

Commoditized by AI: Routine workout math, generic macro counting, automated calendar scheduling.
Human Value Premium: Behavioral empathy, trauma-informed coaching, trust, direct client relationships.
3

Move 3: Ask the AI for the Questions (Deep Angles)

Exploratory Vectoring
MY QUESTION TO AI Polished Academic Speech

"If you were analyzing this business model from every strategic angle—from pricing structures to personal branding—what critical questions would you explore next to reveal the hidden friction points?"

AI STRATEGIC ADVICE & RESPONSE 3 Strategic Vectors

1. The Retention Vector: "What exact client retention mechanisms prevent subscriber churn beyond the initial 90-day transformation window?"

2. The Positioning Vector: "How does an independent coach stand out against low-cost algorithmic apps ($10/mo) while charging premium pricing ($300/mo)?"

3. The Operations Vector: "What specific tech architecture allows a solo coach to manage 50+ clients without experiencing cognitive burnout?"

4

Move 4: Surface Working Hypotheses

Theoretical Framework
MY QUESTION TO AI Polished Academic Speech

"Based on our strategic exploration so far, what core working hypotheses can we synthesize regarding sustainable online coaching success?"

AI STRATEGIC ADVICE & RESPONSE 3 Core Hypotheses
Hypothesis A: Outcome-Based Pricing Model

Coaches who shift from trading hourly time to selling guaranteed lifestyle transformations achieve 3x higher lifetime customer value (LTV).

Hypothesis B: AI as Administrative Multiplier

Using AI tools for routine compliance tracking frees up 70% of coach operational time, directly enabling business scaling without quality degradation.

Hypothesis C: The Authentic Proof Asset

Documented real-world personal training transformation videos and client case studies create an unshakeable moat against commoditized software competition.

5

Move 5: Force Calibration & Confidence Ratings

Quantitative Assessment
MY QUESTION TO AI Polished Academic Speech

"Now force calibration on those hypotheses: assign explicit confidence levels to each, state the core assumptions, and identify key failure risks."

AI STRATEGIC ADVICE & RESPONSE Calibration Matrix
Hypothesis Confidence Underlying Assumption Failure Risk
A: Outcome Pricing HIGH (90%) Clients value clarity over physical gym access. Poor onboarding leads to early dropouts.
B: AI Multiplier HIGH (85%) Coach retains personal touch despite automation. Over-automation feels robotic to clients.
C: Proof Asset MED-HIGH (75%) Audience can distinguish real effort from marketing hype. Market saturation of fake client transformation claims.
6

Move 6: Compress to Reusable Master Prompt

Prompt Synthesis
MY QUESTION TO AI Polished Academic Speech

"Compress our findings into a single, highly structured master prompt that can be reused across any AI model to execute this deep strategy analysis for future business cases."

AI STRATEGIC ADVICE & RESPONSE

// REUSABLE MASTER PROMPT FOR DEEP BUSINESS ANALYSIS

"Act as an elite business strategist and technical co-founder. Conduct an exhaustive feasibility analysis for launching an online coaching business in [Niche Industry]. Address 4 core dimensions: 1) Financial mechanics & high-ticket pricing models required to replace a full-time income; 2) The 5-to-10 year AI disruption horizon, specifying what gets commoditized vs. what retains premium human value; 3) A operational tech stack leveraging AI for client check-ins; and 4) A risk-calibrated launch roadmap with specific failure mitigation strategies."

7

Move 7: Retrospective & Meta-Learning

Meta-Analysis
MY QUESTION TO AI Polished Academic Speech

"What master prompt should I have used from the very start to ask this question? Looking back over our multi-turn exploration, what would have been the single most efficient, structured question to ask from the outset, and what are the top 3 sequential follow-up questions to ask next?"

AI STRATEGIC ADVICE & RESPONSE Retrospective Blueprint

While exploring step-by-step is crucial for learning, you could have collapsed Moves 1 through 5 into this single Master Starting Prompt:

// THE ULTIMATE STARTING PROMPT

"Evaluate the full business viability of operating an independent online fitness coaching business to generate $100k/year. Provide: 1) Financial target ratios; 2) A 5-10 year AI risk analysis; 3) Three testable working hypotheses regarding client retention; and 4) A calibrated confidence matrix detailing assumptions vs. failure risks."

Top 3 Sequential Questions to Ask Next:

Q1:

The Pre-Mortem Test: "Assume 90 days have passed and this online gym launch has failed. What were the exact operational failure points?"

Q2:

Tech Integration: "What exact no-code workflow bridges client workout tracking directly into an automated retention dashboard?"

Q3:

Cross-Model Stress Test: "How would an opposing strategist criticize our high-ticket pricing assumption?"
